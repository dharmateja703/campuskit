Returns policy

We want your calculator, drafter or instrument box to work the moment it arrives. If it doesn't, here's how returns work.

3-day return window

You can request a return for any item within 3 days of it being marked delivered. After that window closes, we're unable to accept returns.

What qualifies

Items that don't power on, have a manufacturing defect, or arrived visibly different from the listing. The item should be unused and in its original packaging where possible.

How to request a return

Go to My Orders, open the order, and click "Request return" on the item within the 3-day window. Include a short reason — our admin team reviews requests and gets back to you.

Still stuck?

Reach out on the contact page and we'll sort it out.
